OUR / PROCESS

THE ART OF THE EXTERIOR

A clearer route from enquiry to completion

From the first enquiry to a clearer scope: six thoughtful steps that explain what a realistic exterior project conversation would involve.

A TRANSPARENT JOURNEY

The decisions that make a project legible.

01

Listen

Clarify the actual problem, the desired change and any constraints before selecting a solution.

02

Survey

Document site context, access, condition and what is not yet known.

03

Develop

Compare practical options and the associated aesthetic and technical trade-offs.

04

Agree

Turn the preferred approach into a legible scope, assumptions and exclusions.

05

Coordinate

Plan communication points, dependencies and required reviews.

06

Review

Revisit the brief and record the hypothetical handover information.
