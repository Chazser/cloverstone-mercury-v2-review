CONCEPT STUDY 001 / NOT A COMPLETED PROJECT

One building. Several competing priorities.

This fictional terrace study explores how an exterior can be considered in terms of material compatibility, weather protection and architectural character. No actual property has been surveyed.

EXISTING CONCEPT VISUAL
PROPOSED CONCEPT VISUAL
01

The setting

A fictional early twentieth-century urban terrace needing a more coherent roofline and sensitive fabric repair strategy.

02

The constraints

Illustrative issues include existing brick character, rainwater routes, neighbouring rooflines and maintenance access.

03

The alternatives

A selective repair could retain more fabric, while broader renewal might improve consistency but introduce greater intervention. Neither is a quoted recommendation.

04

Material reasoning

The visual concept pairs reclaimed-looking brick tones with understated roof finishes and legible water details.

05

Limits

The drawings and images are fictional design studies; no survey, planning approval or construction work has been undertaken.
