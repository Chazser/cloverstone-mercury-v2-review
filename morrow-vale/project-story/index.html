CONCEPT STUDY 001 / NOT A COMPLETED PROJECT

One courtyard. Several competing priorities.

This fictional courtyard study explores exterior materials, weather protection and architectural character. No actual property has been surveyed.

DUSK MATERIAL-REFERENCE VIEW
DAYLIGHT MATERIAL-REFERENCE VIEW
01

The setting

A fictional timber-clad home where shelter, sunlight, a mature tree and weathering create competing design priorities.

02

The constraints

Illustrative issues include the external timber geometry, roof drainage, daylight, weather exposure and maintenance access.

03

The alternatives

A selective repair could retain more fabric, while broader renewal might improve consistency but introduce greater intervention. Neither is a quoted recommendation.

04

Material reasoning

The visual concept pairs warm timber with dark cladding, a restrained roofline and considered drainage junctions.

05

Limits

The drawings and images are fictional design studies; no survey, planning approval or construction work has been undertaken.
