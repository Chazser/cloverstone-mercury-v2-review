Terrace Reconsidered

Repair first. Replace only where justified.

01 / THE DESIGN QUESTION

Understand the site before choosing a finish.

An imagined urban terrace with ageing brick joints, fragmented previous repairs and a roof edge that deserves careful attention.

A constrained street frontage where neighbouring elevations, public access and rainwater paths affect working methods.

Fictional concept exercise. No survey, completed work, client testimonial or conservation approval is represented.

02 / PRIORITIES & MATERIAL CHOICES

Trade-offs worth making visible.

Map the existing materials and moisture pathways before proposing treatments. In a real conservation context, intrusive investigation and heritage permissions would be separately assessed.

01 / MATERIAL DECISION

Compatible mortar

Investigate existing masonry and mortar before recommending an appropriate repair mix.

02 / MATERIAL DECISION

Careful roof junctions

Record water ingress evidence and review lead, slate or compatible alternative detailing.

03 / MATERIAL DECISION

Character retention

Preserve repairable original fabric rather than imposing an unsupported full replacement.

03 / CHARACTER & MATERIALS

Quiet details do the heavy lifting.

Historic brick / natural slate / lime-compatible repair concepts

Visual references communicate atmosphere and material language only. They are not before-and-after photographs, project evidence or proof of construction competence.

Evidence before assurance.
