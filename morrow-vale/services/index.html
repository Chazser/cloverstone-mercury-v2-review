01 / EXTERIOR DISCIPLINE

Rooflines with purpose

Roof design balances structural condition, weather exposure, drainage and the shape of a building. A careful brief records existing defects before selecting a repair or renewal route.

02 / EXTERIOR DISCIPLINE

Masonry & pointing

Good masonry work begins with an understanding of the existing material, moisture movement and the consequences of inappropriate hard repairs.

03 / EXTERIOR DISCIPLINE

Exterior restoration

The right intervention is often the smallest justified one: retain character where possible, document uncertainty and distinguish conservation from replacement.

04 / EXTERIOR DISCIPLINE

Facades & finishes

Cladding and surface finishes must suit the substrate, orientation and exposure. Appearance is one criterion; compatibility and maintainability also matter.
