ESSAY 01 / MATERIAL CULTURE

On objects and room

An object should respond to the space around it. The best composition leaves enough silence for materials, light and proportion to do their work.

Design is a sequence of trade-offs between intention, proportion, context and a practical next step. Understanding those decisions makes an object more useful than an isolated photograph.
