INSIDE THE IMAGINED STUDIO

The shape is only the beginning.

01

01 · Observe

Start with the small physical rituals people repeat: setting a cup down, finding a comfortable glow or displaying a seasonal branch.

02

02 · Draw

Explore volume and line, then compare the silhouettes that express an object’s intended use.

03

03 · Test

Build hypothetical material and finish studies. Describe what would need verification before a real product could be manufactured.

04

04 · Refine

Resolve proportions, care requirements and junction details. A refined concept should communicate its purpose without decorative excess.
