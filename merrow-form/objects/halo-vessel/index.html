01 / THE IDEA

Design begins with a reason.

Designed around a single branch, a spare arrangement or the pleasure of an empty ceramic form.

The wide shoulder tapers into a stable base, creating a contrast between visual lightness and physical grounding.

Original illustrative concept only. No object has been manufactured, independently safety-tested or made available for purchase.

03 / SETTING

A place in the room.

Dining table, sideboard or an uncluttered shelf with indirect daylight.

04 / DEVELOPMENT

Open questions remain.

Glaze safety, ceramic firing shrinkage, mass stability and water-tightness would need physical prototypes and laboratory or supplier verification.

05 / CARE & USE

No invented guarantees.

Dust with a soft cloth. Claims about food use, standing water and dishwasher safety are deliberately withheld.
