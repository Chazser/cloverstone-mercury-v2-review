AN IMAGINED NEIGHBOURHOOD RESTAURANT

A table holds more than dinner.

At its heart, this restaurant concept is about the simple pleasure of taking the time to be together.

01

A place for conversation

The imagined idea for Fenn & Ember begins with the belief that a good meal is measured in time spent together, not how quickly a table turns.

02

Cooking with the season

Our sample culinary language favours vegetables at their best, purposeful seasoning and dishes that feel generous without shouting.

03

The rituals that matter

A warm welcome, a little attention to detail and a room comfortable enough to stay awhile define this fictional restaurant experience.

04

Made to feel familiar

The materials, lighting and menu are designed to belong to the same place: a neighbourhood dining room where the atmosphere is as considered as the plate.
