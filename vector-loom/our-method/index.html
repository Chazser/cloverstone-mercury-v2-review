AN ENGINEERING PROCESS IN CONCEPT

Traceability is a design feature.

A structured engineering method should reveal how assumptions, decisions, risks and verification evidence connect.

01

Requirements

Capture purpose, operating context, constraints and measurable acceptance conditions.

02

Architecture

Identify interfaces, loads, feasible layout envelopes and likely failure routes.

03

Alternatives

Compare feasible concepts with explicit constraints, not aesthetic intuition.

04

Analysis plan

State what must be modelled, measured or independently verified.

05

Review

Bring engineering decisions, risks and evidence gaps together for challenge.

06

Handover

Deliver a clear record of assumptions, revisions and next verification steps.
