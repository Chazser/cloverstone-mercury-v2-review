ENGINEERING AS A DISCIPLINE

Design decisions, made visible

Three fictional engineering problems explored through design constraints, competing alternatives, uncertainty and proposed verification.

THREE FICTIONAL TECHNICAL EXERCISES

Show the reasoning, not merely the result.

CONCEPT 01 · HYPOTHETICAL

High-temperature bracket

A support bracket must tolerate a hypothetical hot enclosure and periodic thermal cycling.

ILLUSTRATIVE ENGINEERING REFERENCE

Design constraints

Thermal gradients, bolted interfaces, stiffness and assembly access.

Concept alternatives

Cast geometry / machined assembly / formed sheet.

Evidence and verification

Define operating temperatures; assess thermal expansion and load paths; test representative cycles. No test or analysis is represented as performed.

CONCEPT 02 · HYPOTHETICAL

Compact fluid enclosure

An illustrative enclosure needs service access while managing sealing and compact packaging.

ILLUSTRATIVE ENGINEERING REFERENCE

Design constraints

Joint compression, thermal movement, inspection and replacement.

Concept alternatives

Split housing / monolithic body / bolted cover.

Evidence and verification

Document pressure and temperature envelope; verify sealing through planned bench tests before any claim of suitability.

CONCEPT 03 · HYPOTHETICAL

Lightweight machine support

A hypothetical structure seeks lower mass without making assembly or inspection impractical.

ILLUSTRATIVE ENGINEERING REFERENCE

Design constraints

Stiffness, fatigue sensitivity, cost and manufacturing variation.

Concept alternatives

Sheet frame / tubular assembly / ribbed plate.

Evidence and verification

Compare candidate stiffness assumptions with an appropriately validated prototype and load test.
